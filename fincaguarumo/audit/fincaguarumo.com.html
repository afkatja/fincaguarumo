Welcome to Finca Guarumo

Forest of Birds

Off-Grid Eco-Lodge & Sustainable Vacation Rental in Costa Rica's Osa Peninsula

Experience Costa Rica's biodiversity when you book your sustainable vacation rental near Corcovado National Park. Located 6km from Puerto Jiménez, our off-grid rainforest lodge is the perfect rural tourism escape for spotting wildlife, birdwatching, and authentic nature experiences in the Osa Peninsula's pristine jungle.

Off-grid sustainable rainforest Lodge and Wildlife Sanctuary

Experience sustainable luxury at Villa Bruno, our solar-powered eco-villa surrounded by primary rainforest, where scarlet macaws visit daily and howler monkeys serenade you to sleep. Perfect for photographers, nature lovers, and eco-conscious travelers seeking authentic Costa Rica experiences.

Working with local guides, we offer curated rural tourism nature tours, including forest hikes and our popular cacao farm tour experience.

Finca Guarumo supports reforestation and biodiversity conservation. Your sustainable stay directly helps fund the planting of native trees and the protection of habitats in this biologically rich region.

Prime Osa Peninsula Location

Within 15 drive from Puerto Jiménez. Minutes from secluded beaches and less than 1 hour drive to Corcovado National Park. Our jungle location offers tranquility while maintaining easy access to the region's top wildlife viewing areas.
