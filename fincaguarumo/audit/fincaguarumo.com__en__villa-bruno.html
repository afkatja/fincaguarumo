About this accommodation

An off-grid, eco-friendly and sustainable lodge with easy access to the town of Puerto Jimenez, with its shops and restaurants. Villa Bruno at Finca Guarumo offers a two-bedroom accommodation with a garden and terrace. Guests enjoy free WiFi, sea and mountain views, solar-powered hot shower, and a fully equipped kitchen.

Continue reading to learn more about this unique eco-villa experience...

Wake to howler monkey calls and birdsong in our spacious, solar-powered eco-villa. Villa Bruno is Finca Guarumo's sustainable vacation rental, combining sustainable luxury with authentic Costa Rican wildlife experiences, no park tickets required. We are the ultimate jungle stay and a true farm-to-table vacation.

Wildlife at Your Doorstep

Villa Bruno's elevated position and proximity to the primary rainforest mean extraordinary wildlife viewing without leaving the property:

Daily Sightings (90%+ probability)

- Scarlet macaws (usually in pairs, morning and evening fly-overs)
- Squirrel monkeys (troops of 10-20 individuals)
- White-faced capuchin monkeys
- Chestnut-mandibled toucans
- Fiery-billed aracari
- Coatis

Frequent Visitors (60-80% probability)

- Howler monkeys (you'll definitely hear them!)
- Carolina (our resident brocket deer)
- Agoutis
- Various hummingbird species
- Mot-mots, trogons, tanagers, and many other avian species

Occasional Special Guests

- Tayra (weasel-like carnivores)
- Collared peccaries
- Anteaters
- Tapirs

Best Viewing Times: Dawn (5:00-7:30 AM) and dusk (4:30-6:00 PM)

Getting to Villa Bruno

From San José

- Drive: 6-7 hours via Costanera Sur (Route 34)
- Fly: 45-minute flight to Puerto Jiménez (daily SANSA flights)
- Bus: Direct daily buses from San José to Puerto Jiménez

From Puerto Jiménez

- Distance: 6km (15-minute drive)
- Road Conditions: unpaved road, some rough patches
- Vehicle: Any car works in the dry season; 4x4 recommended during the rainy season
- Transportation: Available from the airport ($20 USD)
- GPS: only use Waze

Directions from Puerto Jiménez

  • Follow the national main road 245 towards the south when exiting Puerto Jimenez
  • Drive 2 km, do NOT cross the bridge
  • Turn right at the sign Finca Guarumo
  • Drive 4 km uphill
  • The gate to Finca Guarumo is on your left
  • Open the gate and park your vehicle in the covered car park
  • You will see Villa Bruno right next to the car park

Airport Pickup

We can arrange pickup from Puerto Jiménez airport for $20 USD

Location and access

How do I get to Villa Bruno from Puerto Jiménez?

We're just 6km from Puerto Jiménez, accessible by any car (4x4 recommended during rainy season May-June, October-November). We will provide a detailed description after your booking.

Power and connectivity

Is Villa Bruno really off-grid with reliable electricity?

Yes! Our solar system provides stable power year-round for lights, fans, cooking, and device charging, even during Costa Rica's rainy season

Will I have internet and phone access in the Osa Peninsula?

We use Starlink satellite internet with high-speed Wi-Fi, ideal for Zoom, streaming, and remote work. Cell signal is very limited, so we recommend using Wi-Fi calling or messaging apps.
